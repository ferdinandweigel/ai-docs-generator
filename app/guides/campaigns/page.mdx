# Campaigns

### Campaign Creation, Audience Selection, and Scheduling

Create your campaign by defining its objective, content, and delivery channels. Next, select your target audience using filters such as demographics, behavior, and engagement history to ensure precise targeting. Finally, schedule your campaign by choosing the optimal date and time for launch, with options for immediate or automated future delivery.
